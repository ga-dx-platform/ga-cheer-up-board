# Product

## Register

product

## Users

GA company employees (Thai-speaking). They open the board during work hours: on a break, after a positive moment with a colleague, or when they want to share an idea. Context is casual — not a task they came to complete, but a moment they chose to create. They post anonymously or with their name, read what others have shared, and react to messages they connect with. Seniority varies; every role in the company is a potential reader and poster.

## Product Purpose

A shared office noticeboard for positive communication. Employees post thank-you messages, ideas, and cheer-up notes. The board surfaces these for the whole team to read, react to, and feel good about. Success looks like employees genuinely wanting to open it — not because HR asked them to, but because it makes them feel connected to the people they work with.

## Brand Personality

Warm · Playful · Encouraging. The voice is a friendly colleague, not a company communications channel. Light, celebratory, safe to post in. Never corporate, never competitive.

## Anti-references

- **Social media feed**: No algorithmic sorting, no visible engagement scores, no "trending" signals. Reactions exist to amplify warmth, not to create popularity contests.
- **Gamified dashboard**: No leaderboards, no contribution streaks, no badges. Encouragement should not become competition. The most appreciated message and the least-reacted one sit with equal dignity.

## Design Principles

1. **Warmth over performance** — every decision that would make the board feel like a performance metric (reaction counts as status, sorted by popularity) works against the purpose. Default to chronological, equal-weight presentation.
2. **Every voice has equal weight** — anonymous and named posts receive identical visual treatment. The UI never implies some voices matter more than others.
3. **The tool disappears into the feeling** — submitting a message, reacting, reading: these should feel frictionless. The interface steps back so the human content is the focus.
4. **Joyful but not loud** — playfulness lives in the aesthetic (soft gradients, glass, emoji, gentle motion), not in aggressive color or overwhelming decoration. Delight should feel like a gentle exhale, not a shout.
5. **Celebration of small moments** — the board is for everyday appreciation, not just big milestones. The UI should feel light and quick to use, never precious or formal.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Thai and English copy coexist; Kanit handles both scripts. Keyboard navigation for all interactive elements. Minimum 44×44px touch targets on mobile. Reduced-motion alternative for all animations. Focus-visible rings on interactive elements.
